# `all_properties` on the r4133 channel — the vendored G1.1 census

Evidence base for `R4133_PROPS_PLAN.md` (the GOLDEN_REBASE G1.1 successor).
Every row of every table that plan introduces — normalization rule, echo
exclusion, enum synonym, shape allowlist row — has to trace back to a row in
this directory (plan §1.1 mechanic (a), "evidence-first"), and the RP2.1 replay
test reads `examples_full.txt` + `bins.tsv` directly.

Landed by **RP0.1**. These files are **frozen data**: the only test over them
today is the integrity lock `crates/dss-core/tests/props_r4133_evidence_lock.rs`
(row counts, cross-file consistency, SHA-256 over the five verbatim copies —
added by RP0.1's audit round because a lost or edited row here would weaken the
RP2.1 replay silently instead of failing it). RP2.1 is the first consumer of
their *content*. Nothing here is generated by `cargo test`.

The full census is re-derivable — **not** by re-running a scratch test, but with
the permanent knob `DSS_PROPS_CENSUS=1` that RP0.2 landed on the corpus gate
(2026-08-22; procedure in `TESTING.md`). As landed it writes
`tmp/props_census.json` + `tmp/props_census/run.json` and, per channel,
`structural_pairs` / `numeric_pairs` / `shape` / `summary` / `examples_full` in

need the §1.1 bin policy and the in-scope case filter that only RP2.1's

the knob escapes uniformly in every extract (`\\` for backslash, `\x27` for an
apostrophe), where the 2026-08-08 generator escaped `structural_pairs.txt` only
(see the data traps below).

**cell** counts are counts of census rows (one element × prop × step).

## Row formats and how to parse them

`structural_pairs.txt`, `numeric_pairs.txt`, `examples_full.txt` and the two
`*_in_scope.txt` files are all of the shape

```
class.prop | 'left' | 'right' | <number>[ | <number>]
```

**Parse with the quote anchors, never by splitting on `|`.** The reliable regex
is

```
^(\S+) \| '([^']*)' \| '([^']*)' \| (.*)$
```

because values may contain `|` (see the traps) but never contain `'`, CR, LF or
TAB — asserted over the whole census when these files were generated.

* `structural_pairs.txt` / `structural_pairs_in_scope.txt` —
  `class.prop | rust-example | r4133-example | rows`.
* `numeric_pairs.txt` / `numeric_pairs_in_scope.txt` —
  `class.prop | rust-example | r4133-example | max_rel | rows`.
* `examples_full.txt` — `class.prop | rust | r4133 | count`: **one row per
  distinct `(rust, r4133)` spelling per pair**, values **untruncated**, sorted by
  pair (structural pairs first, then numeric) and within a pair by descending
  cell count. The 3 378 rows' counts sum to 1 055 446 = every value cell in the
  census (960 129 structural + 95 317 numeric). This is the file RP2.1's replay
  pushes through the r4133 policy; every pair carries **all** of its spellings
  here, foldable booleans and echoes alike — including the nine bin-1 pairs whose
  r4133 side is an echo rather than a boolean (§"Bin 1 carries nine echo pairs,
  not three", which corrects the plan's count).
* `bins.tsv` — tab-separated,
  `pair · kind · bin · subbin · cells · cells_in_scope · max_rel · max_rel_in_scope`.
  `kind` ∈ `structural`/`numeric`, `bin` ∈ 1..7, `subbin` is `case`/`trail` for
  bin 2 and `-` elsewhere, the two `max_rel` columns are `-` for structural pairs
  (and `max_rel_in_scope` is `-` for a pair with no in-scope cell).

## `bins.tsv` — the assignment rule

Every census pair is assigned to **exactly one** bin of `R4133_PROPS_PLAN.md`
§1.1 (bin 8 is the property-table shape gap: it lives in `shape.txt`, has no
pairs, and is not in `bins.tsv`). The rule below reproduces §1.1's per-bin pair
**and** cell totals exactly — that equality is the acceptance criterion of
WP-RP0 and was verified at generation time.

**Structural pairs (bins 1–5).** Classify the pair's *representative cell* — the
pair's **first row in census order**, which is exactly the cell the
`structural_pairs.txt` example columns print — through this chain, first match
wins:

1. **bin 1 — boolean rendering.** The Rust side is `Yes` or `No`. The port always
   renders FPC `Yes`/`No`; r4133 answers with one of eleven Delphi spellings
   (`true`/`True`/`false`/`False`/`YES`/`yes`/`no`/`NO`/`n`/`y`/`Y`) or, where its
   `GetPropertyValue` has no arm, with the `PropertyValue[]` echo (`''`, or a
   stale parse string such as `relay.reset`'s `0.20`). The `''` cells inside this
   bin are echoes, not booleans — plan §1.1 routes them to the echo table, which
   is why a bin-1 pair may legitimately hold both a `BoolFold` and an echo claim.
2. **bin 5 — empty-vs-value / display defaults.** Either side of the
   representative cell is `''`.
3. **bin 2 — case-only + trailing space.** The two sides are equal
   case-insensitively (`subbin = case`), or equal after also trimming outer
   whitespace (`subbin = trail` — the literal `'wye '`/`'Delta '` of
   `Transformer.pas`/`AutoTrans.pas`).
4. **bin 4 — array form.** Either side starts with `[` or `(`: the dss_capi
   `GetDSSArray` form vs r4133's comma/paren/bare forms.
5. **bin 3 — enum spelling + singletons.** Everything else: the per-pair enum
   spellings (`Positive`/`Pos`, `Positive`/`pos`, `Schedule`/`UNKNOWN`,
   `none`/`kft`, `close`/`open`) and the per-pair semantics
   (`monitor.mode`'s `17` vs its `1 16 +` decomposition render — **corrected by
   RP2.2**: that string is the deck's RPN *source text*, not a decomposition;
   see §"What RP2.2 moved"). Only 4 of the 8 pairs turned out to be synonyms;
   the other 4 are echoes or a live-state divergence, per that same section.

Order is load-bearing: bin 1 before bin 5 keeps the mixed boolean pairs out of
the echo bin; bin 5 before bin 4 keeps `line.wires`-style `[]`-vs-`''` pairs out
of the array bin; bin 2 before bin 4 keeps `autotrans.conns` (a case-only diff
that happens to be bracketed) in the case bin. Deciding on the representative
cell — rather than on the pair's whole cell population — is deliberate: it is the
same cell the vendored pair extracts show, so a reader can re-derive any bin from
`structural_pairs.txt` alone. What that costs — a label that does not describe
every cell of its pair — is the subject of the two sections below the numeric
rule, and it is not confined to bin 1.

**Numeric pairs (bins 6–7).** `bin = 7` iff the pair's `max_rel` over the whole
census is **≥ 1e-4**, else `bin = 6`. The threshold is not tuned: the census has
an **empty band** — the largest display-precision pair peaks at `6.43e-5`
(`load.pf`) and the smallest genuine jump starts at `1.00e-3`
(`invcontrol.lpftau`), so every cut inside `(6.43e-5, 1e-3]` produces the same
partition. Re-derived over in-scope cells only (`max_rel_in_scope`), the same
threshold gives 37 / 16 — the numbers §1.1 states, and the reason RP2.4 derives
its floor from the in-scope extract only (out of scope,
`storagecontroller.kwneed` peaks at `1.38e-3` and is still display-class).

Note that the bin follows the *rendering mechanism*, not the triage's §N2 prose:
`autotrans.kv`/`kva`/`kvs`/`kvas`/`normhkva` are named in the triage's
genuine-jump paragraph but peak at `~5e-6` and are bin 6 here, which is what
§1.1's count of 61 requires.

## The in-scope filter

`structural_pairs_in_scope.txt` and `numeric_pairs_in_scope.txt` re-derive the
pair extracts over the cells the RP4.1 unmask will actually compare.

**Filter.** Keep a census cell iff its case's manifest entry has
`engines ∈ {"both", "r4133"}` — i.e. the case is gated on the r4133 channel.
Cases with `engines: "capi_v0145"` are dropped whole (plan §1.3: their r4133
property behavior is deliberately out of scope). A pair survives iff it keeps at
least one cell; its `rows`/`max_rel` are recomputed over the kept cells only, and
its example is the **first kept cell** (so an in-scope example may differ from the
full-census example of the same pair — `structural_pairs.txt` shows the first
cell overall).

**The case-label → manifest join.** A census `case` label is
`"<manifest>:<path>"`:

* prefix `solvable_now:` → the entry with that `path` in
  `tests/corpus/manifests/solvable_now.json` (294 cases: 235 `both`, 40 `r4133`,
  19 `capi_v0145`);
* prefix `asymmetric:` / `controls:` / `modes:` → the entry with that `path` in
  `tests/corpus/<family>/manifest.json` (52 / 105 / 70 cases).

The two sides join **exactly**: all 438 census case labels resolve, and the union
of the four manifests is the 521-case population of
`tests/corpus/manifests/population.lock.json` (366 `both` + 96 `r4133` + 59
`capi_v0145`), so the filter admits 462 of the 521 cases.

**The filter is `engines`-only, and that is one notch wider than what actually
runs.** Four of the 462 (`modes:inputformat/shape_binfiles/shape_binfiles.dss`,
`solvable_now:Test/IEEE13_LineAndCableSpacing.dss`,
`solvable_now:Test/IEEE13_LineSpacing.dss`,

## Known data traps

* **Values contain `|`.** OpenDSS separates matrix rows with `|`, so
  `line.cmatrix` (12 cells), `line.rmatrix` (1) and `line.xmatrix` (1) carry
  values such as `[72.7194481250695 |0 72.7194481250695 |0 0 …]` — 13 rows of
  `examples_full.txt`, one row of `numeric_pairs.txt` and one of
  `numeric_pairs_in_scope.txt`. A naive split on `|` mis-parses them; use the
  quote-anchored regex above.
* **Element names contain `|` too.** In the local full census the `element`
  column holds `Line.b1||b2` (`modes:reduce/reduce_mergeparallel.dss`, the
  merge-parallel deck). The vendored files carry no element column, but anything
  reading the census JSON must not split element names on `|` either — take the
  class as the prefix before the **first** `.`.
* **The vendored pair files truncate their examples.** `numeric_pairs.txt` cuts 9
  example cells at 34 characters, over 8 pairs (`autotrans.kvas`,
  `autotrans.kvs`, `line.cmatrix` — both sides, `transformer.kvas`,
  `transformer.kvs`, `vsource.puz0`/`puz1`/`puz2`); `structural_pairs.txt` cuts 3
  cells over 2 pairs (`energymeter.mask` both sides at 40 chars,
  `storage.dynadll` at 46) and escapes its backslashes (`\` → `\\`).
  Whole values live **only** in `examples_full.txt`, the two `*_in_scope.txt`
  files (both untruncated and unescaped) and the local census. A tokenizing or
  numeric comparator fed from `*_pairs.txt` would compare a truncated string.
  The escaping asymmetry also means one cell is spelled **two ways across the
  frozen extracts**: `storage.dynadll` reads `C:\\Users\…` in
  `structural_pairs.txt` but `C:\Users\…` in `examples_full.txt` — RP2.1's
  replay reads the latter and must not assume the pair-file spelling (the RP0.2
  knob, by contrast, escapes uniformly in every extract it writes).
* **One pair name is both structural and numeric.** `isource.bus1` appears in the
  structural set (`'pcc'` vs `'PCC'`, bin 2) *and* in the numeric set (`'b2'` vs
  `'b2.1'`, bin 7). `bins.tsv` disambiguates with its `kind` column;
  `examples_full.txt` has the prescribed four columns only, so join its rows on
  `(pair, rust, r4133)` — the triple is unique across the whole file.
* **`max_rel` is rounded** to two significant decimals (`%.2e`) in every extract,
  as in the G1.1 originals. Exact per-cell values are in the census.
* **`bins.tsv` labels a pair, it does not classify its cells** — 17 pairs are
  heterogeneous and the label is order-dependent (§"A pair's bin is a label"),
  and bin 1 holds nine echo-carrying pairs rather than the plan's three
  (§"Bin 1 carries nine echo pairs"). Both traps bite the RP2.1 replay first.
* **The `bin` column is a full-census label; the in-scope numeric split is
  re-derived.** `max_rel_in_scope >= 1e-4` moves four bin-7 pairs
  (`capacitor.cuf`, `generator.kvar`, `storage.kw`,
  `storagecontroller.kwneed`) into the display class, which is how the
  full-census 61 / 33 becomes the in-scope 37 / 16. Counting the `bin` column
  over in-scope pairs instead gives 33 / 20 — the wrong pair of numbers.
* **These extracts are frozen evidence**, not a live measurement: they describe
  the 2026-08-08 tree. Any later re-measurement belongs to the RP0.2 knob, and a
  disagreement between the knob's output and these files is a finding, not a
  reason to overwrite them.

## Why this tree is not in the golden lock

`crates/dss-core/tests/golden_lock.rs` fixes its scope by enumeration
(`GOLDEN_REBASE_PLAN.md` §1.2) and this directory is deliberately outside it: no
row here is engine output or an oracle capture the suite compares against, none
is regenerated by any `tools/golden/*.py`, and the lock's anchor vocabulary has
no value for "frozen census extract". The decision is recorded where a reader of
that lock will find it — `golden_lock.rs::EXCLUDED_TREES` — and the bytes are
guarded instead by `props_r4133_evidence_lock.rs`: SHA-256 over the five
verbatim copies, exact row counts for the derived files, and the cross-file
equalities (`bins.tsv` ↔ the pair files ↔ `examples_full.txt` ↔ the in-scope
files ↔ `shape_in_scope.txt`), so a dropped or edited row fails a test instead
of quietly shrinking what RP2.1 proves.
