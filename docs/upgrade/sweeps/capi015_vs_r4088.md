## Headline reading

Two very different divergence populations sit here:

1. **Text-only / discrete-ordering diffs** where the *electrical state is
   identical* (V ≈ 1e-13…1e-15). These are dss_capi-vs-EPRI **format**
   differences already in the ledger/known_diffs: event-log wording, monitor
   header whitespace, control-queue enumeration order. They confirm capi015
   reproduces r4088 *logic*.
2. **Genuine FPC-vs-Delphi numeric drift** on a handful of decks — the ledger
   candidates.

### Correction (2026-09-03, RP3.12) — the AutoTrans row is not FPC-vs-Delphi

The reading above (`V 2–3e-2 @ atf.3`, `regcontrols differ: ['rat']`) is right; the
diagnosis "a discrete reg-tap resolves differently" is wrong, and it is the
conditioning excuse CLAUDE.md forbids. **EPRI does not resolve the tap one step
later — it never resolves one at all.** Its `RegControl` reaches the controlled
element through an unchecked `TTransfObj(ControlledElement)` typecast
(`Version8/Source/Controls/RegControl.pas:926`, `:1026`, `:1296`, `:1370`,
`:1479`) while `TAutoTransObj = class(TPDElement)`
(`Version8/Source/PDElements/AutoTrans.pas:88`) is not a `TTransfObj`, so
`TapIncrement` reads the winding's `MaxTap` (1.1 pu) and
`PendingTapChange := Round(BoostNeeded/Increment)*Increment` (`:1249-1250`) zeroes
every realistic boost. Measured live on r4133: **0** event-log lines on all four
`controls:autotrans/*` decks against 10–13 on the port/0.14.5 oracle, the
regulated bus left 2.0–2.6 V outside its band with 5–11 taps of headroom unused,
3–7.5 % on `AutoTrans.WdgCurrents` winding 2 — EPRI's numbers are the
**unregulated** circuit. Present identically in r3723, r4088 and r4133.

The ledger cause key `autotrans-regcontrol-tap` was renamed to
**`regcontrol-autotrans-typecast`** and rewritten with this diagnosis
(`tests/corpus/ledger.json`). The port keeps the regulated answer in both lanes
(upstream bugs are never reproduced) and is pinned by
`props_r4133_pins::autotrans_wdgcurrents_stay_regulated_where_r4133_never_taps_the_autotrans`.
Upstream report:
`investigations/to_opendss/50-regcontrol-autotrans-ttransfobj-typecast.md`.
