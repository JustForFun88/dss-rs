# Sweep: `capi015` (0.15.0b4/r4103) ↔ `oddie:r4088` (10.2.0.1) — FPC vs EPRI @ r4088 line

Isolates dss_capi(FPC) vs EPRI(Delphi) at the *same* upstream line (0.15.0b4 ≈
SVN r4103; r4088 = Columbus). This is the **DIVERGENCES ledger cross-check**
(L1–L4) and the `delta_r3723_r4088.md` FPC-vs-Delphi surface. **378 cases: 323
match, 24 diverged, 31 error** (24 capi015 strict-validation `error_a`, 7 r4088
`error_b`).

> **Caveat — not a pure FPC-vs-Delphi discriminator.** capi015 (0.15.0b4) tracks
> ≈ SVN **r4103** with changelog cherry-picks to **r4119**, so the pair also
> carries the ~15–30-revision r4088→r4103/r4119 *version* deltas on the capi015
> side (e.g. `TCC_Curve.none` is r4119, `AllocateLoad` disabled-skip r4115),
> **not only** compiler drift. A subset of the numeric divergences below
> (autotrans reg-tap, makeposseq drift) could be part version-delta rather than
> pure FPC-vs-Delphi last-ulp. The magnitudes are tiny so the ledger reading
> holds, but do not over-trust this pair as a clean compiler-only isolation.

## Headline reading

Two very different divergence populations sit here:

1. **Text-only / discrete-ordering diffs** where the *electrical state is
   identical* (V ≈ 1e-13…1e-15). These are dss_capi-vs-EPRI **format**
   differences already in the ledger/known_diffs: event-log wording, monitor
   header whitespace, control-queue enumeration order. They confirm capi015
   reproduces r4088 *logic*.
2. **Genuine FPC-vs-Delphi numeric drift** on a handful of decks — the ledger
   candidates.
